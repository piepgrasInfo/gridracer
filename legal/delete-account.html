Deleting your Grid Racer account

Effective date: 2026-10-02

Published at: https://piepgras.info/legal/grid-racer/delete-account/

Grid Racer is developed by Martin Kruse, operator of https://piepgras.info. You can reach us at apps@piepgras.info; our postal address is listed at https://piepgras.info/imprint/.

A Grid Racer account is optional. It exists only for online play: multiplayer races, the rating leaderboard, and the shared custom-track library. You can play the whole game without one. If you made one, you can delete it, and you do not need the app installed to ask us to.

Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Open Grid Racer and go to Settings.
  2. In the Server section, make sure you are logged in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Tap Delete account…, enter your password to confirm, and tap Delete account.

The password goes to our server over an encrypted connection. Versions of the app before 1.3 send it unencrypted, so update the app first if you can.

The account is deleted immediately. If you are in a race or a lobby at the time, leave it first — the app will say so.

Ask us to delete it

Write to apps@piepgras.info with the subject "Delete my Grid Racer account" and tell us the username of the account. You do not need to write from any particular address and you do not need the app installed.

If we cannot tell that the request comes from the account holder, we may ask you to confirm something only the account holder would know — for example by sending the request from the app while logged in, or by logging in once after we ask. This is to stop somebody else deleting your account.

We delete the account within 30 days of the request, and normally within a few days.

What is deleted

Deleting the account removes, permanently and without a recovery period:

  • your username, display name and password;
  • your rating, match count and win count, and your row on the leaderboard;
  • your session tokens, so every device you were logged in on is logged out;
  • the custom tracks you shared on our server, and the ratings and reports other players filed on them;
  • the ratings and reports you filed on other players' tracks.

Your name and account handle are also removed from the race logs our server keeps. The races themselves are kept, because a race log belongs to everyone who drove in it — your seat is renamed and no longer points at any account. Race logs are deleted entirely 30 days after the race in any case.

What is not deleted

  • Data on your device — your settings, local leaderboard, custom tracks and saved replays. These never leave your device and are not part of the account. Uninstalling Grid Racer, or using Clear data in Android's app settings, removes them.
  • Backups you control — if Android's backup is enabled, a copy of the app's local data may exist in your Google account. Remove it in your device's backup settings.
  • Crash reports, if you turned crash reporting on. They carry no account identifier, so they cannot be matched to an account automatically; write to apps@piepgras.info and tell us what you can, and we will delete what we can identify.
  • Data we must keep for a legal obligation, or to defend a legal claim. We do not currently hold any account data on that basis.

After deletion

The username becomes available again for anyone to register, and the deleted account has no claim on it. You can create a new account at any time; it starts with a fresh rating and no history.
