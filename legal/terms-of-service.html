Automated limits

Some rules are enforced automatically, without a person looking at it first, so that one client cannot spoil a race or a server for everybody else:

  • Turn timeout. If it is your turn in an online race and you do not move before the room's time limit runs out, your turn is forfeited and the race continues without you. The host sets this limit for the room (5, 10 or 20 seconds; 20 unless they change it) from the lobby before the race starts. The remaining time is shown on screen to everyone in the room, not just whoever is moving, while a turn is running.
  • Placement timeout. If you do not choose a starting slot within 45 seconds, one is chosen for you.
  • Idle rooms. A room where nothing happens is closed — after 30 minutes in the lobby, or 15 minutes during a race.
  • Rate limits. Requests are limited per connection and per network address. Exceeding a limit drops the request, not the connection; you will see a "slow down" message.

None of these are penalties, and none of them affect your account: they exist because everyone else in the room is waiting.
